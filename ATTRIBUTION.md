# Data, media and research attribution

- **Natural Earth 1:110m land polygons:** `public/data/land.png` is a rasterized locator generated from [Natural Earth's `ne_110m_land.geojson`](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson). Natural Earth data is [public domain](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). Modern coastlines are geographic context, not historical borders.
- **Historical claims:** `public/content.js` cites the Metropolitan Museum of Art, UNESCO World Heritage Centre and the Nara National Research Institute for Cultural Properties with item-level links and source scopes. The original summaries are project prose; those source websites and any media on them retain their own rights.
- **Fonts:** System UI fonts and Georgia/Times fallback; no font files are bundled.
- **Illustrations and photographs:** None in this edition. The 2D globe is rendered at runtime from the Natural Earth mask. No parent project texture, film, generated historical scene or archival map is included.

The MIT software license applies to the app code. It does not change the terms of linked sources or third-party data.
