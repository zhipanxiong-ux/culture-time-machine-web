ABOUT THIS ATLAS

A time machine
with footnotes.

Choose a moment in history. Explore different places. Discover how people lived and why their worlds were different.

Our public edition begins with one reviewed comparison: the capital regions of Tang China and Nara Japan around 750 CE. It draws on a larger private desktop archive, but does not claim that the rest is ready to publish.

What a date means

“750 CE” is a comparison point inside two longer histories. Sources rarely describe every household in a single year. Dates, geographic names and social positions are kept as specific as the evidence allows. A modern coastline is a locator, not a historical border. The web atlas ends its research horizon at 2025; it does not invent later history.

How evidence is shown

Topic cards link directly to source institutions. Each comparison calls out what those sources cannot establish. The Nara palace tablets offer direct evidence of some deliveries and offices, while the Tang essay gives a broad view of a connected capital. These have different levels of detail.

Help correct the record

Historical corrections should identify a place, period, current claim, proposed change and supporting evidence. Use the public repository’s issue template. Submissions are reviewed before changes appear.

The globe uses public-domain Natural Earth 1:110m land polygons. Coastlines are modern geographic context; no historical frontier is drawn. No generated historical illustration is included in this edition.
